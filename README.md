# Women’s Rights School Campaign – Mollahat Upazila

This repository documents my learning journey and a school-based awareness campaign focused on gender discrimination and female rights.

## 📍 Campaign Overview
I organized a school campaign in Mollahat Upazila with Class 9 girl students to raise awareness about gender discrimination and women’s rights.

## 🎤 Activities Conducted
- **Speech Competition:**  
  Students delivered speeches about women who inspire them.

- **Problem & Solution Analysis (Group Work):**  
  Students discussed everyday problems faced by females and proposed practical solutions.

- **Poster & Placard Activity:**  
  Students created posters expressing female voices that amplify women’s rights and promote gender equality.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Raise awareness about female rights  
- Encourage critical thinking among students  
- Empower young girls to express their voices confidently
